## 1. Executive Summary

This slice implements bounded, source-backed local food research conforming to Spec 0007:
1. Reuses the existing evidence collector task structure, query planning ceiling, and SerpAPI/Scrapling pipeline.
2. Distinguishes regional dishes (`local_dishes`) from physical dining candidates (`dining_recommendations`).
3. Enforces attributable dish-to-venue pairings: venues are paired with dishes only when supported by co-occurring, non-conflicting observation content.
4. Enforces destination city constraints with alias expansion (`mysuru` <-> `mysore`), excluding wrong-city venues.
5. Keeps unsupported pricing, hours, and dietary claims strictly unknown (`None`, with `unavailable_details` explicitly marking them as `UNKNOWN`).
6. Ensures food suggestions remain optional (`is_must_visit = False`) and never displace or convert traveler must-visits.
7. Retains food research findings when routing is unavailable or degraded.

Verification is supported by a dedicated regression suite (`tests/unit/test_source_backed_local_food_research.py`, 7/7 passing), broader regression suites (127 passing tests), live direct runner verification on Bengaluru -> Mysuru, clean Ruff checks, and clean Mypy type verification.

**Status: APPROVED**
