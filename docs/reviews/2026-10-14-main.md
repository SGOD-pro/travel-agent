## Nits

- ⚪ `backend/src/functions/evidence_collector/template.yaml:65`: In `EvidenceCollectorFunction`, `CodeUri: ../../` points to `backend/src/`. The standalone `requirements.txt` is located at `backend/src/functions/evidence_collector/requirements.txt`. In standard `sam build` environments using standalone pip rather than `uv`, SAM looks for `requirements.txt` at the root of `CodeUri` and falls back to `PythonPipBuilder:CopySource`. When configuring production deployment pipelines, consider symlinking `requirements.txt` to `backend/src/` or configuring a custom Makefile builder or AWS Lambda Layer.
