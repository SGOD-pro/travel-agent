## Summary
This change implements Slice 8A for specification 0007, delivering a bounded search backed vertical slice across query planning, real task dispatch, collector execution, browser fallback, receipt deduplication, and graph resumption. Query planning in `QueryPlanningService` deterministically formulates at most 4 destination research queries covering attractions, authentic dining, affordable accommodation, and route advisories, mapping them to a durable collector budget of 4 tool calls and 200 minor currency units in Indian Rupees. `EvidenceCollectorService` executes collection tasks under worker lease fencing and durable budget reservations. When dynamic pages emit `NEEDS_BROWSER`, `TripPlannerGraph` caps browser escalation at at most 2 URLs for this flow without altering general worker settings. `CompletionReconciliationService` deduplicates completion receipts and resumes execution. Before candidate evaluation, `ingest_claims_from_observations` derives structured claims and links genuine observations with explicit support relationships, feeding supported places into `DeterministicEvidenceEligibilityGate` without pre seeded database claims. Lodging observations strictly preserve `INDICATIVE_SEARCH` classification and are never promoted to `LIVE_OFFER`. Route advisories with absent hazard alerts are marked `ADVISORY_UNKNOWN` rather than `SAFE`, maintaining fail closed safety.
