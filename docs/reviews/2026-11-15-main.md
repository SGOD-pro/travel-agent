## Summary
This review confirms the implementation and verification of the food and accommodation research slice under spec 0007 Slice 8. The implementation plans bounded searches using trip destinations, dates, preferences, and budget tier context while strictly adhering to the 4 query ceiling and 200 minor INR cost limit.

Source backed food and accommodation candidates are collected, preserved, and partitioned into the proposal output with honest provenance. Culinary dishes are explicitly distinguished from physical business venues, preventing food items from creating false attraction existence claims. Dining venues carry source URLs, retrieval times, identities, and uncertainty states.

2. Actual browser dispatch, execution, reconciliation, and graph consumption remains PENDING when static extraction succeeds and emits no dynamic rendering requirements.

## Findings Status

### RESOLVED: Query planning incorporates dates, preferences, and budget within bounds
In `backend/src/functions/orchestrator/evidence/query_planner.py`, `plan_destination_queries` extracts start date, traveler preferences (dietary and culinary keywords), and budget tier (BUDGET, MODERATE, LUXURY). It plans at most 4 bounded queries per destination across attractions, food, accommodation, and travel alerts, staying strictly within the 200 minor INR budget.

### RESOLVED: Distinction between culinary dishes and business venues
In `backend/src/functions/orchestrator/evidence/place_extraction.py`, `is_culinary_dish` filters out traditional preparations (such as Kathi Roll, Rosogolla, Macher Jhol, Kosha Mangsho) from physical place candidate accumulators. In `claim_ingestion.py`, dish names are excluded from physical attraction existence claims. `extract_local_dishes_from_observations` compiles authentic regional dishes with `is_business_venue: False`, source URLs, retrieval times, and editorial discovery uncertainty.

### RESOLVED: Indicative accommodation search without fabricated offers
In `scrape.py`, `extract_page` parses hotel cards and marks them with `evidence_class=INDICATIVE_SEARCH`, `is_indicative_search=True`, `is_verified_offer=False`, `booking_confirmed=False`, and `taxes_unknown=True`. In `claim_ingestion.py` and `graph.py`, lodging claims and proposal outputs preserve indicative search classification, unpriced hotels remain None without zero coercion, and explicit notices declare that live supplier offers, room availability, booking confirmations, and cheapest hotel claims are not included.

### RESOLVED: Provenance carried into proposal and API response
Candidate proposals, eligible stops, and editorial recommendations preserve source URLs, retrieval times, identities, uncertainty, unavailable details, and conflicting details in provenance. In `trips.py`, `ItineraryResponse` exposes `food_research` and `accommodation_research`.

### RESOLVED: Full running graph tracing to proposal
In `test_trip_planner_evidence_runner_lifecycle.py`, an offline integration test executes GraphRunnerService, stages authentic observations, reconciles collection and route optimization tasks, and proves proposal synthesis with local dishes, dining recommendations with provenance, indicative lodging options, and exclusion of unrelated commercial offices.

### RESOLVED: Separately gated connected acceptance scenario
In `test_trip_planner_evidence_connected.py`, live SerpAPI search with 4 bounded queries produces 16 observations, 15 claims, and 15 evidence links on isolated PostgreSQL. The running graph evaluates candidate eligibility, advances through route solving, and synthesizes proposal output with verified dining recommendations, authentic regional dishes, and indicative lodging options carrying explicit notices, while keeping unsupported control candidates unverified and preserving both pending gates.
