## Verification of Requirements

### 1. Bounded Advisory Query Planning

In `backend/src/functions/orchestrator/evidence/query_planner.py`, `plan_destination_queries` formulates targeted queries:
1. When origin is present, query 4 covers: `{origin} to {primary_dest} road travel advisory route conditions weather road closures {date_context}`.
2. When origin is omitted, query 4 covers: `{primary_dest} road travel advisory route conditions weather warnings road closures {date_context}`.
3. Total queries remain strictly bounded to at most 4 queries and 200 minor units INR budget.

### 2. Advisory Claim Ingestion and Field Preservation

In `backend/src/functions/orchestrator/evidence/claim_ingestion.py`, Section 4 ingests advisory and restriction observations:
1. Observations representing road closures, weather warnings, landslides, cyclones, or floods are identified from artifact categories, types, or excerpt content.
2. Authoritative publishers and domains (`.gov.in`, `.nic.in`, `imd.`, `ndma.`, `nhai.`, `police.`) evaluate `is_authoritative = True` and receive higher claim confidence (0.95 vs 0.75).
3. Claims preserve `advisory_type`, `severity`, `headline`, `affected_area`, `applicable_start`, `applicable_end`, `observation_id`, and `source_url`.
4. Road closures and travel restrictions are ingested under category `RESTRICTION`; weather warnings and cyclone alerts are ingested under category `WEATHER`.

### 3. Geographic and Temporal Relevance Filtering

In `backend/src/functions/orchestrator/graphs/trip_planner/graph.py`, `_build_advisory_research` evaluates relevance:
1. Spatial relevance matches affected areas against origin, destinations, corridor highways, and scheduled stops. Advisories for unrelated corridors (e.g. NH-10 in Sikkim during a Kolkata to Digha trip) are quarantined to `unrelated_advisories`.
2. Temporal relevance compares applicable end dates with trip start dates. Ended alerts are classified as `expired_advisories` with uncertainty set to EXPIRED.
3. Conflicting reports on the same corridor are preserved in `conflicting_advisories` with uncertainty set to CONFLICTED.

### 4. Fail Closed Safety and Honest Status Reporting

In `graph.py`:
1. Absence of warnings NEVER evaluates to SAFE.
2. When relevant active warnings exist, status evaluates to `ADVISORIES_PRESENT` with a count of active warnings.
3. When conflicting warnings exist, status evaluates to `ADVISORIES_PRESENT` with reason `Conflicting travel advisory reports detected for route corridor`.
4. When evidence is expired, status evaluates to `ADVISORY_UNKNOWN` with reason `Advisory evidence is expired or lifted; real-time road conditions unmonitored`.
5. When all evidence is for unrelated corridors, status evaluates to `ADVISORY_UNKNOWN` with reason `No verified advisories for route corridor during travel dates; unmonitored road segments remain unverified`.
6. When no evidence was retrieved, status evaluates to `ADVISORY_UNKNOWN` with reason `No road hazard or travel advisory evidence retrieved for route; route safety unverified`.
7. All responses carry the safety notice: `Absence of travel advisories or warnings is not proof of safety; unmonitored conditions remain unverified.`

### 5. Proposal Output Integration

In `synthesize_proposal_node`:
1. Proposal attaches `advisory_research`, `advisories`, and `advisory_status`.
2. Critical and warning advisories append informational notices to `route_diagnostics` without altering route geometry or inventing unverified closures.

### 6. Focused Offline Regressions

In `backend/tests/integration/test_claim_ingestion.py`, five targeted tests verify:
1. `test_advisory_research_relevant_active_warning`: Proves active relevant road closure is preserved with full provenance, authoritative flags, and route diagnostics.
2. `test_advisory_research_unrelated_area_excluded_and_reports_advisory_unknown`: Proves warning for unrelated corridor is excluded, reporting ADVISORY_UNKNOWN without claiming safety.
3. `test_advisory_research_expired_warning_treated_as_expired_not_safe`: Proves expired advisory evaluates to ADVISORY_UNKNOWN, not SAFE.
4. `test_advisory_research_conflicting_reports_preserved_honestly`: Proves contradictory reports are preserved side by side as CONFLICTED without guessing.
5. `test_advisory_research_unavailable_evidence_reports_advisory_unknown`: Proves empty evidence returns ADVISORY_UNKNOWN with explicit reason and safety notice.
