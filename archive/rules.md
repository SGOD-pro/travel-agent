# rules.md

1. Build contracts before product code.
2. Test every module independently before composition.
3. The minimum required product set is the default.
4. Never run all three products merely because they exist.
5. Suggestions are not execution.
6. LLM reasons; it does not own deterministic authority.
7. LAYA makes bounded decisions; Policy remains authoritative.
8. Never invent prices, availability, locations, routes, weather, events, opening hours, closures or bookings.
9. Unknown cost is not zero.
10. Hard route constraints cannot be silently removed.
11. External web/media content is untrusted.
12. Never bypass CAPTCHA, authentication, anti-bot controls or access restrictions.
13. Browser workers are bounded.
14. Every task is idempotent.
15. Every retry/repair loop has hard limits.
16. Every meaningful operation is traceable.
17. Never log secrets/tokens.
18. Provider registry status must be respected.
19. Local fixtures do not prove live provider availability.
20. PostgreSQL is authoritative state.
21. S3 holds large artifacts.
22. Redis is never the only source of truth.
23. Route optimization authority is OR-Tools + routing data.
24. Verification is evidence-based.
25. A model prediction is not automatically verified truth.
26. Do not expose internal workers as public APIs.
27. Prefer reuse of verified old code, but do not preserve obsolete architecture.
28. Record architectural changes in decision.md.
29. Travel research must consider all relevant destination-intelligence categories before final synthesis; “relevant” is determined by request, destination, time window, traveler and evidence availability.
30. Weather, festivals, events, road restrictions, closures, opening hours and availability are time-sensitive; store observed/published time and freshness status.
31. Hidden/lesser-known status is a label with evidence requirements, not a model fact.
32. Do not recommend private, restricted, dangerous or illegal-access locations as ordinary travel experiences.
33. Local food and restaurant recommendations must distinguish a food/dish claim from a specific business claim.
34. Famous/must-visit is not synonymous with “best”; preserve source basis and explain ranking signals.
35. Nearby is evaluated using practical travel time/access, not only straight-line distance.
36. Photoshoot/scenic recommendations must preserve accessibility and time-of-day applicability when known.
37. Do not research every category blindly; expensive/current-data tools are gated to relevant tasks.
38. A destination recommendation without sufficient evidence must remain a candidate/uncertain insight, not verified truth.
